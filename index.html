01The studio

I make people stop scrolling. Five seconds of a Chicken Licken cut or a Galxboy drop does more selling than a page of copy — so that's the standard every Kaeduas frame is held to.

Kaeduas runs on one person and a full stack of machines. Qophe "QJ" Motsamai — born in Bloemfontein, Free State on 4 June 2005 — directs, generates, edits, designs and codes the work himself. No account managers, no hand-offs, no telephone game between a brief and the finished thing.

Advertising is the proving ground — spec concepts built for the brands that set the bar: streetwear and food meta ads, a cinematic sunscreen spot, a collab film study for Luxe Homme × Kabza De Small. Self-initiated, unaffiliated, and honest about it — every frame generated, cut and finished in this studio. When the cameras are off, QJ builds software — research agents, chatbots, sites like this one.

05Brief me

Answers, before you even ask.

Who is Qophe Junior Motsamai?

QJ — born in Bloemfontein on 4 June 2005 — is a creative AI architect and the founder of Kaeduas. He directs every piece of work the studio ships, from the first frame to the final commit.

What does Kaeduas actually do?

Generative video and AI advertising for Meta, YouTube and TikTok. Brand and campaign design. And the software side: portfolio sites, research agents and chatbots — the Systems section above is all real, public code.

How do I book the studio?

Use the brief form below, or email motsamai.main@gmail.com with your brief. In a hurry? WhatsApp +27 67 236 3235 — the same number rings his phone.

What does it cost to work with Kaeduas?

Engagements come three ways: a paid pilot (one brief, one deadline — judge the output before committing to anything bigger), a fixed-scope project, or a monthly retainer. Every quote starts from the brief, and the pilot exists so you can measure the value before you scale it.

Does Kaeduas work white-label with agencies?

Yes — that's a native mode. The agency owns the client relationship; Kaeduas is the AI production unit behind it, turning briefs into finished work. Retainer or project-by-project, whichever fits how you buy.

Can Kaeduas invoice? Can procurement handle this?

Yes. Kaeduas invoices as an independent contractor in ZAR or USD, remote from Bloemfontein, South Africa (SAST, UTC+2). Contracts, NDAs and the usual paperwork are all fine.

Is the work shown here client work?

No — the ads in the work section are spec concepts: self-initiated, unaffiliated studies built for brands the studio admires. They exist to show craft and range. Commissioned work gets the same treatment, with your name on it.

Where are you based — and do you work with international clients?

Born and raised in Bloemfontein, South Africa — working worldwide. The studio runs remote-first, which means your timezone is a detail, not a dealbreaker.
