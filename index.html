The founder
Qophe Junior
Motsamai.
Born Bloemfontein. Based Johannesburg. Building Kaeduas as a creative house — one person, four pillars, one brand.

Kaeduas is a sole proprietorship trading as a creative house, not an agency — the word is too small and too rented-out.

The work spans four things: Build (brands, systems, platforms, products), Create (design, film, sound, writing, art direction), Entertain (content, shows, formats, culture), and Inform (teaching, documenting, demystifying). Scale runs underneath all four as the operating engine.

Everything here is public by default. The process is the product.
