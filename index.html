I make clear, distinctive visuals that earn attention. From a printed piece to a social-first film, every detail has a job.

I’m QJ, a graphic designer and creative producer based in South Africa. I work hands-on from concept to final artwork, bringing people, photography, design and contemporary image-making together to tell clear brand stories.
